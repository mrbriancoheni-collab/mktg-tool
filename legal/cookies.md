AdCanvas uses only **strictly necessary** browser storage, which is needed for the Service to work. Because these items are essential, they don't require consent under EU/UK ePrivacy rules.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|

| `adcanvas-client-id` | Local storage | Random ID used to show who else is viewing a board | Until cleared |

| [SESSION COOKIE NAME] | Cookie | Keeps you signed in (once authentication is enabled) | [Session / 30 days] |

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

**If you add analytics or marketing tools** (for example Google Analytics or a chat widget) to the app or to your marketing site, update this table. In the EEA and UK you must also add a consent banner that blocks those tools until the user opts in.

You can clear these items at any time in your browser settings. The app will still work, but it will forget your preferences.

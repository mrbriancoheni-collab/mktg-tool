# Data Deletion Instructions

You can delete your data from AdCanvas at any time. This page is also the data deletion URL we give to platforms such as Meta and Google.

## Delete data yourself (immediate)

1. Open AdCanvas, then open the **board menu** (the grid icon next to the campaign name).
2. Choose **Privacy & data → Delete everything**. This permanently deletes all boards, comments and AI assets, and disconnects every integration, which deletes the stored access tokens.

To remove only one platform connection, select that integration on the canvas and click **Disconnect**. Its stored credentials are deleted right away.

## Revoke access from the platform side

You can also remove AdCanvas's access in each platform's settings:
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram):** Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → AdCanvas → Remove.
- **Google:** [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) → AdCanvas → Remove access.
- **LinkedIn, TikTok, HubSpot, Shopify and others:** remove the app from the connected-apps settings in that platform.

When access is revoked, we stop syncing data, and the stored credentials stop working. Delete your data in AdCanvas as described above to remove previously synced metrics.

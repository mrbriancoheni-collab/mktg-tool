# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy applies to everyone who uses AdCanvas. If you break it, we may suspend or terminate your access under the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## You may not use the Service to:

**Create unlawful or deceptive advertising**
- Make false, misleading or unsubstantiated claims, including health, financial, earnings or "get rich quick" claims.
- Use fake reviews or testimonials, or endorsements without the disclosures required by law (such as the FTC Endorsement Guides).
- Run discriminatory targeting or exclusions in housing, employment, credit or other protected categories, or get around platform rules for special ad categories.
- Promote illegal products or services, or advertise regulated products (such as alcohol, gambling, cannabis, pharmaceuticals or financial products) without the required licenses and compliance with platform and legal rules.
- Impersonate a person, brand or organization, or create content meant to mislead people about its origin.

**Misuse personal data or messaging**
- Upload special-category or sensitive personal data (health, biometric, precise location, children's data, government IDs, financial account numbers) or customer lists, unless the Service expressly supports it.
- Send email or SMS without the consent and opt-out mechanisms required by law (such as CAN-SPAM, TCPA, GDPR/PECR or CASL), or buy, sell or scrape contact lists in violation of law or platform terms.
- Track or profile individuals in violation of privacy law.

**Abuse the Service or connected platforms**
- Break platform terms of the ad networks and tools you connect, including getting around their review, rate limits or policies.
- Probe, scan or test vulnerabilities, or bypass security, authentication or usage limits, without written permission.
- Reverse engineer the Service, or use it to build a competing product.
- Upload malware, or interfere with the Service or other users.
- Resell the Service, or share accounts, except as your plan allows.

**Misuse AI features**
- Generate content that is illegal, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit involving minors, or violates others' intellectual property.
- Try to extract our prompts or models, or use outputs in violation of our AI provider's usage policies.
